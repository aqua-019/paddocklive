# SP-06 · Fitz Feral Hunters · v1.2: one Contact button; reviews with stars, photos and verified jobs (diff-mode SP, D700 §0D)

Compiled against: STAMP :: D700 · 4 Oct 2026 · 6511 entries D0001-D6558 · 218 laws · 387 capsules · 466 groups · 1824 pairs · 499 anti-pairs · 457 palettes · 21 lenses
Measured state before the diff: SP-05, 29/29 VERIFIED.
Brief (Aqua, 10 Nov 2026):
- Contact and Request a quote were two top-nav items doing the same job. Make one gold button that says "contacting", and let the other pages keep their own buttons to the quote form.
- Add a reviews page where Fitz's clients can:
  - leave 1 to 5 stars
  - write at least 250 characters
  - attach photos
- Give the site a way to verify that a reviewer had a job with Fitz.

## Audit (before)

- **Nav:** a "Contact" text link and a gold "Request a quote" button both led to /contact. Reviews wasn't in the nav at all (footer and home only).
- **Reviews:**
  - text only, 20 to 800 characters
  - no rating and no photos
  - "a quick check that you're a customer" with nothing to check against beyond an email address
- **CLAUDE.md:** said "no star ratings".

## Delta

**Nav**
- One gold **Contact** button at the end of the nav and the drawer. It's `aria-current` on /contact.
- Reviews takes the freed nav slot, so there are still seven links.
- Every on-page "Request a quote" button (hero, tiles, call bar, footer) still goes to `/contact#enquiry`.

**Star rating** (D3778)
- Five radios, so arrow keys work.
- Each label is a 48px cell; the stars fill gold through `:has()`.
- Hover previews in colour only.
- A visible readout, e.g. "4 out of 5 · Very good".
- The hint says every rating is published.

**Review text** (D0312)
- 250 to 2,000 characters, with a live count ("212 of 250 characters, 38 to go").
- The server says how many characters the review has.

**Job month**
- A select of the last 24 months plus "More than two years ago".
- The script rebuilds the list from today's date, so it never goes stale.

**Phone**
- Optional and private, to help Fitz match the job.

**Photos** (D0335)
- **Picker:** up to 3 photos, each with a preview and a Remove button. It's shown only when the private Blob store is connected (`GET /api/reviews` → `photos:true`).
- **Browser:** each photo is redrawn to ≤1600px JPEG on a canvas. This drops EXIF and GPS and keeps 3 photos under Vercel's 4.5 MB request limit.
- **Server:** checks every photo again and removes APP1–APP15 and COM segments. The photos go to a **private** Vercel Blob store.
- **`/api/review-photo` serves a photo only when:**
  - its review is published with photos, or
  - the request carries Fitz's moderation signature.
- Otherwise it returns 404.

**Verifying the job**
- **Review link:** Fitz texts the customer a signed link (HMAC with REVIEW_SECRET, single use, 180 days). It pre-fills the form, and the review arrives marked as a verified job.
- **Where Fitz gets review links:**
  - every enquiry email carries one ready-made for that customer
  - the bottom of every review email links to his private page `/review/invite` for making more, with Text it / Email it / Copy
- **Otherwise:** the moderation page won't publish until Fitz ticks "I've matched this to a job I did". If he can't match it, he declines it as "Not a customer".
- **Public card** (D1150): a green "Verified job" chip, with a tooltip saying which way the job was verified.

**Cards and summary**
- Each card shows:
  - the stars as `role=img`, "Rated N out of 5"
  - the chip
  - up to 3 photo thumbnails, each linking to the full photo
  - "Job Nov 2026"
- The average is always shown with its count ("4.0 out of 5 · from 1 review") on /reviews and on home.
- No AggregateRating schema, because Google treats a business's own reviews as self-serving.

**Moderation page**
- Shows the stars, the job status, the job month and the private phone, plus signed thumbnails.
- Has a "Show the photos too" box, so Fitz can publish the words without a photo that shows a location, a number plate or a face.

**Policy text**
- "Good or bad, whatever the star rating", "matched to a real job first", and the photo rule.

**Code**
- `@vercel/blob` 2.8.2 (pinned) is the first runtime dependency, used only for review photos.
- `api/_lib/site-options.js` is generated by the build, so the functions and the form share one list of services.
