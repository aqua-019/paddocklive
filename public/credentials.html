Paperwork

Certificates and currency documents are supplied on request, before work starts. Licence and membership numbers are shared with you directly rather than published here.

Codes and permits

All work follows the relevant Victorian codes of practice for the humane control of each species, and every animal is dispatched to the standard of AHCPMG304, a nationally recognised unit.

Deer are a game species in Victoria and are taken under a Game Licence. Kangaroo control on private land needs an Authority to Control Wildlife (ATCW) from the Victorian government; I can help with the application.
